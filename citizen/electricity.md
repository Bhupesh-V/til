# Electricity

A large part of delhi is either on BYPL or BRPL (part of BSES).

## Meter Name Change

URL: https://ncbypl.bsesdelhi.com/BYPL/ChangeRequestLogin

- You should update the phone-number and email on the bill before updating the name, this is handy if you want to update the details later on, update the mobile first.
- Make sure
  - You have a NOC from the person your co-owning the property with.
  - Upload the whole chain of GPA.
  - Your ID proof should be self-attested.
- **Important**: If you make a mistake you cannot cancel the request online, you will have to visit the local office or call them up, so please be careful.
